01 / A little introduction

About the
wordsmith.

A thoughtful mix of commercial copywriting and editorial sensibility.

Curiosity first.
Clarity always.

I'm Aakriti, a copywriter with experience across beauty marketing, brand communication and publishing. I enjoy finding the idea at the heart of a brief and shaping it into writing that feels distinctive, useful and true to the brand.

At ESGA, I work on Sephora campaigns across email and social channels, translating campaign inputs and creative direction into audience-facing copy. My favourite part of the process is developing fresh concepts and building the first draft from the ground up.

My experience with Prakash Publications adds an editorial perspective to the way I approach language: with attention to detail, structure, tone and the reader.

04 / My approach

From brief
to better words.

Good copy starts well before the first sentence is written.

01. Understand

Get clear on the brief, audience, objective, inputs and non-negotiables.

02. Explore

Find the strongest angle, tone and creative route for the communication.

03. Create

Write and refine copy for the channel, format and brand voice.

04. Polish

Check clarity, accuracy, consistency, character limits and the final details.
